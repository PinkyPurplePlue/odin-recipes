Pizza Recipe

Description

This homemade pizza recipe is simple and quick to prepare! It's simply delicious!

Ingredients

  • pizza dough
  • tomato sauce
  • melting cheese
  • any toppings you would like

Steps

  1. Prepare your dough, it could be storebought or you can prepare your own if you can! Shape your dough to a circle and flatten it.
  2. Add your tomato sauce, don't add a lot or it will get soggy. Spread it evenly.
  3. Now add your melting cheese, or even any cheese you would like.
  4. Now add your favourite toppings! My favourite is pepperoni. You can add anything you want, go nuts!
  5. Bake it in your preheated oven of 190 degrees C for about 10 minutes or until the cheese is melted.
  6. Cool for 5 minutes. Serve and Enjoy!
