My Sandwich Recipe

Description

This homemade sandwich recipe is simple and quick to prepare! It's my go-to if I get bored of rice meals. It's simply delicious!

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of wheat bread loaf
  • 1 tablespoon of sandwich spread
  • 1 tablespoon of cheese
  • 2 slices of ham
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp of cooking oil

Steps

  1. Preheat your pan on low-medium heat with a tsp of oil.
  2. Cook your ham 2 mins per side or longer if thicker slice.
  3. While your ham cooks, prepare your bread and put sandwich spread on both bread slices.
  4. Set your cooked ham on 1 bread slice.
  5. Add another tsp of oil to the pan, and fry 2 eggs sunny side up. Add your cheese on top so it melts.
  6. Stack your egg and cheese and finish setting up your sandwich.
  7. Let it cool for 3 minutes. Serve and Enjoy!
