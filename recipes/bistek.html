My Bistek Recipe


Description

This homemade bistek recipe is simple and quick to prepare! It's simply delicious! It's one of my favorite homemade dishes and this recipe is the usual bistek recipe you can find anywhere just a little altered in some ingredients. This recipe calls for 1-2 servings.


Ingredients

  • 250 ml soy sauce
  • 250 g of beef
  • 6-8 calamansi squeezed juice
  • 5-7 cloves of garlic minced
  • 2 red or white onions, ring sliced
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil


Steps

  1. Slice your beef into bite sizes.
  2. Prepare your marinade by mixing the soy sauce, calamansi juice, minced garlic, and 1 tsp of blackpepper.
  3. Place your beef into a container then pour the marinade. Make sure the marinade covers all of the beef. Let it marinate for 30 mins or overnight in the fridge.
  4. Preheat your pan with 1 tbsp of cooking oil on medium-high for 3 mins. Place your marinated beef and cook it for 5 mins both sides. Don't toss away the marinade!
  5. Set aside the cooked beef. Turn the heat on medium place another tbsp of cooking oil and cook half of your sliced onions until softened and its aroma is very fragrant.
  6. Place the cooked beef back to pan again together with the marinade.
  7. Mix it and let it simmer for 10 mins.
  8. Top it off with the rest of the sliced onions.
  9. Serve with hot rice and Enjoy!
