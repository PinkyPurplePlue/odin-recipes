My Pancity Canton with Taco Meat Recipe


Description

This pancit canton recipe is my go to when I crave its nostalgic taste from my childhood, but I also want it to pack some yummy protein. Simple and quick to prepare! It's simply delicious! This recipe calls for 2-3 servings.


Ingredients

  • 2 packs of Pancit Canton kasalo pack, or 4 packs of the normal size Pancit Canton, any flavor you want but I prefer the chilimansi.
  • 500 g of ground beef
  • 1 pack of taco seasoning
  • 2 white onion diced
  • a handful of lettuce sliced and diced
  • 1 bell pepper diced
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil


Steps

  1. Preheat a large pot in medium-high heat with 1 tbsp of cooking oil. Cook your ground beef until it browns.
  2. Mix your taco seasoning with beef and cook for another 3 mins. Set aside the taco beef.
  3. Turn the heat to medium and sautee your diced onions, bellpepper, and lettuce until the diced onions softens. Set aside the vegies.
  4. Cook the Pancit Canton in the same pot by the instructions on the pack.
  5. Mix everything together in a large plate.
  6. Serve hot and Enjoy!
